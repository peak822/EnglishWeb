# Daily Life Short Stories Design

This document outlines the addition of 4 new short stories to the `Short Story Reading` section. All stories will focus on the "Daily Life" genre to provide relatable, everyday vocabulary for learners.

## Proposed Stories

### 1. Morning Coffee (Level: A1)
* **Genre:** Daily Life
* **Read Time:** 1 min
* **Content Summary:** A very simple story about waking up, making a cup of coffee, looking out the window, and getting ready for work. Focuses on basic morning routine vocabulary.

### 2. The Supermarket Trip (Level: A2)
* **Genre:** Daily Life
* **Read Time:** 2 mins
* **Content Summary:** A story about going to the grocery store with a shopping list. The character navigates aisles, asks for help finding an item, and pays at the cashier. Focuses on food and shopping vocabulary.

### 3. A Busy Day at the Office (Level: B1)
* **Genre:** Daily Life
* **Read Time:** 3 mins
* **Content Summary:** Describes a typical workday. The character attends a team meeting, replies to emails, and has lunch with coworkers. Focuses on professional environment and communication vocabulary.

### 4. Weekend Chores and Relaxation (Level: B2)
* **Genre:** Daily Life
* **Read Time:** 4 mins
* **Content Summary:** A story about balancing weekend responsibilities like cleaning the house and doing laundry, followed by taking time to read a book and relax in the evening. Uses more complex sentence structures and descriptive vocabulary.

## Technical Changes
* Add the 4 new JSON objects to `src/data/stories.json`.
* Ensure each story has `id`, `title`, `genre`, `level`, `readTime`, and `content` fields matching the existing structure.
